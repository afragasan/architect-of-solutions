Most portfolio sites hide the seams. You see the finished case study, not the seventeen decisions that got made along the way — which content layer to use, whether to enforce structure at build time, what to leave out of v1 entirely.

This site is going to do the opposite. The repository behind [alejandrofraga.com](https://alejandrofraga.com) is public, and this blog will document the real decisions as they happen: why Work entries enforce a fixed seven-section structure at build time instead of trusting each write-up to include a trade-offs section, why there's no database in v1, why the newsletter lives on a third-party platform instead of being built from scratch.

The premise is simple: for a Solutions Architect, showing the trade-offs behind a decision is a better signal than showing the decision alone. That applies to the site itself, not just the case studies on it.

More posts in this series will follow each meaningful architectural decision as the site grows past this initial scaffold.
